World news in one image — Monday, 23 November 2026.

A make.fun entry for the bounty
[news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123):
one image that sums up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026.

The image is a newspaper-style front page led by a full-width hero
story — Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang as chief executive, 11 days
after he resigned, and its shares jump 31% — above seven more
photo stories from that day: Ukraine sinks the Russian corvette
Merkury off Benghazi with sea drones, Iran says it downed a US
drone east of the Strait of Hormuz, Indonesia names its two
vice-presidential picks, the EU proposes a 22nd sanctions package
on Russia, South Korea's education minister quits over an AI exam
cheating scandal, German media report Russia plans a nuclear-armed
satellite, and India pledges €1.4 billion to help Nepal rebuild
after the deadly glacier flood — plus a ticker with Asian chip
stocks sliding, the US seeking extradition of "Chapo Isidro", and
US Treasury sanctions on Iran's oil tanker networks. Headlines and
summaries use large type and every summary is at most 12 words, so
the page still reads at small sizes. The photos are AI-generated
illustrations (no real press photos, no identifiable people); all
text is rendered with Python PIL and DejaVu fonts, and every fact
is verified against reporting dated 23 November 2026.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so both runs write identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```
